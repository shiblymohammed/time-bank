# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Municipality administrators and coordinators are the primary users of the Time Bank web dashboard. They review membership applications, coordinate volunteer assistance, and resolve issues in their assigned organizations.

Super admins oversee the platform across municipalities. Merchants have their own role-specific workflows for offers and redemptions. Members, volunteers, and caregivers use the separate mobile app for participation.

## Product Purpose

Kottakkal Time Bank helps eligible community members find practical help from verified volunteers. The web dashboard helps staff take that work from membership approval to matching, visit completion, and follow-up. A successful dashboard session lets staff see what needs attention, take the permitted action, and confirm its saved outcome.

## Positioning

Each municipality manages its local services, members, and requests within a centrally governed platform. Time credits earned through volunteering belong to the individual and remain in one wallet across participating organizations. Merchant loyalty coins and offers use a separate system.

## Operating Context

- Municipal staff review applications and supporting information, grant approved roles, manage local services and wards, and handle request and matching exceptions.
- Time-sensitive work includes instant help, safety alerts, and complaints. Staff also review credit activity and report on operations within their access scope.
- Super admins provision organizations, monitor platform-wide activity, and handle global restrictions and oversight.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Dashboard access depends on current approved roles. The backend enforces organization scope and permissions for each action.
- Staff views use live API records and show the outcome of saved actions. A selected municipality determines which local records staff are working on.
- Keep membership and role decisions distinct. An application for a role does not grant that role.
- Use the product terms *time credits* and *loyalty coins* consistently, with units and balances clear wherever they appear.

## Product Principles

1. Make the next action clear for municipal staff, especially when a case is urgent or blocked.
2. Show which organization and role a decision applies to before staff act.
3. Make saved outcomes and reasons visible so staff can follow up or hand off a case.
4. Keep time credits and merchant loyalty activity distinct throughout the experience.
